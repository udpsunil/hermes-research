## 💡 FREE ALTERNATIVES TO TRY FIRST
Before paying IEEE, check:
- **USENIX FAST conference pages** — VST (FAST'18), Amber (FAST'20), SoftSSD (FAST'22) are FAST papers and often freely available
- **Google Scholar** — click "All versions" under a paper; author-hosted PDFs are common
- **arXiv** — search paper titles; preprints are often posted
- **Your employer's IEEE Xplore subscription** — most storage companies have one
- **Semantic Scholar (semanticscholar.org)** — aggregates free versions
